## The Idea

### Motivation

With the rising trend of **short-term** and **vacation rentals**, many large hotel chains are facing increasing difficulty in attracting younger
travelers who prioritize flexibility and unique experiences. Laureate Club seeks to bridge this gap by offering a **luxury travel membership**
tailored to the preferences and demands of the younger, high-end traveler.

This presents a **win-win** situation for both hotels and travelers alike. There's a growing demand for luxury travel among young professionals,
and they are often willing to pay for a membership that offers exclusive benefits, such as discounted rates and priority access.

Given that this demographic tends to travel at least three times a year on the lower end, they would greatly value the savings and premium
experiences that a membership offers. On the flip side, hotels and airlines are keen to attract younger customers, who represent a key segment
for building long-term loyalty. By offering discounts and tailored experiences through the Laureate Club, these service providers can foster
a new generation of loyal customers while ensuring that the luxury travel needs of younger travelers are met. This dynamic not only benefits
travelers seeking exclusive deals but also helps hotels and airlines expand their customer base, ensuring sustainable growth in a competitive
market.

### The Solution

Laureate Club was aimed to be both a **mobile application** and a **web platform** that allowed users to access exclusive deals and benefits
from partner hotels, airlines and other luxury service providers. The app featured a clean, user-friendly interface that showcased the
latest deals and promotions, as well as a membership portal where users could manage their account details and preferences.
