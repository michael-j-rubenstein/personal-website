## The Idea

### Motivation

I was in a pretty dark place when the idea for **FOMO** first came to me. Some personal
challenges had left me feeling low, but oddly, they sparked a drive to make a change.
Unfortunately, that motivation was fleeting, and soon I found myself slipping back into
the same cycle of procrastination.

One day whilst taking a shower, I was reflecting on why I struggled to stay productive.
**Why did my motivation always fade so quickly?**

Then, I thought back to my university days. Even though the workload was **overwhelming**
and the assignments were never-ending, I always managed to push through. The reason? I had
a whole network of peers around me, all facing the same pressures. If I didn't keep up, I'd
fall behind, and the thought of missing out on the success or progress of others kept me
going. It was that underlying sense of falling behind, the sense of **FOMO** which became a
powerful motivator.

And that's when it hit me: **What if I could harness that same feeling and transform it into
motivation?** And just like that, the idea for the FOMO App was born.

### The Concept

The **FOMO App** is a productivity tool that leverages the power of social accountability
to ensure that you stay on track with your goals. The concept is simple: you set a few
goals for yourself, everytime you complete a task, you take a picture and the task completion
status along with the image gets sent to a chat with your friends.

Your friends all receive a notification when you complete a task, but the catch is that they
can't see what you've done until they've completed a task themselves. This creates a sense
of **FOMO** - the fear of missing out - and motivates your friends to complete their tasks
so they can see what you've done.

## Tech Stack

### Frontend

The frontend of the application is developed using **React Native**, enabling a seamless
cross-platform mobile experience. To streamline the development and build processes, **Expo**
is employed for efficient workflow management. For state management, **Redux** is utilized in
combination with **AsyncStorage** to ensure smooth data handling and persistence.

Currently, the app is undergoing Beta testing on iOS. To sign up for testing, please visit
the [FOMO website](https://fomoapp.ca/).

### Backend (Initial Release)

For fast development, the backend is currently deployed on **Firebase**, utilizing **Firestore**
for NoSQL database management and **Firebase Cloud Functions** for serverless computing. The
authentication system is powered by **Firebase Auth**, ensuring secure user management and data
protection. **Firebase Cloud Storage** is used for image storage, allowing seamless integration
with the frontend. Additionally, the **Expo Push Notifications API** is employed to deliver
real-time notifications.

### Backend (Future Plans)

Firebase is a great platform for initial development, but as the user base grows, it may become
necessary to migrate to a more scalable solution. In the future, the backend will be
transitioned **TBD**.

## Application Preview

The application was designed to follow a minimalistic and intuitive user interface, focusing
on ease of use and functionality. Below are some screenshots of the app in action.

### Login Flow

The **Login Flow** is designed to be **simple** and **straightforward**, allowing users to
quickly access the app and start setting goals.

<MainImage src="/images/blog/fomo/Login Demo.png" alt="Login Flow" />

### Tasks Page

This screen is kept **private** to the user, where they can view their tasks for **Today** and
**Tomorrow**.

The design focuses on providing a **fast** and **intuitive** experience, allowing users to
quickly add tasks without being overwhelmed. If they wish to make changes or set recurrences,
they can easily do so by clicking on the task itself.

There are two main types of tasks: **Daily Tasks** and **Recurrent Tasks**.

**Daily Tasks** must be completed within the same day they are assigned. On the other hand,
**Recurrent Tasks** are tasks that need to be completed a certain number of times each week.
These tasks reset at the start of a new week, giving you a fresh opportunity to tackle them.

<MainImage src="/images/blog/fomo/Tasks Demo.png" alt="Login Flow" />

### Chat Page

Welcome to the **social** side of the app, where the **FOMO** happens! On this page, users can
see the tasks their friends have completed and the images they've shared. However, access
to this content is unlocked only once they've completed a certain number of their own tasks.

Users are notified whenever a friend completes a task, triggering a sense of **FOMO** that
motivates them to catch up and view their friends' accomplishments.

<MainImage src="/images/blog/fomo/Chat Demo.png" alt="Login Flow" />

Interactive features on this page include **Sending Emojis** and **Commenting**. Users can
quickly send an emoji by either double-tapping the post or by clicking the Emoji Icon in the
bottom right corner, which opens an **Emoji Panel**. This feature allows users to easily
express support or encouragement while adding to the **FOMO** by seeing posts with lots of emoji
reactions from friends. For more options, users can customize their emojis by clicking the plus
icon on the **Emoji Panel.**

The chats are designed to be for you and your close friends, therefore they are currently limited
to have only **6 members**. Users can join a chat via the chat **secret code**, which is a unique
5 digit code that is generated when a user creates a chat.
